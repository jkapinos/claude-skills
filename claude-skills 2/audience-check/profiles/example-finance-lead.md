# Audience profile: Riley Chen, Finance Lead (fictional example)

**Role:** Finance Lead at Northwind Outfitters, a fictional retailer. Owns month-end close, revenue reconciliation, and vendor payments.
**Last updated:** 2026-10-21

## How they read
- Reads Slack on their phone between close tasks; skims the first two lines and decides whether to open the rest later.
- Prefers short bullets over paragraphs. Long messages during close get saved "for later," which usually means next week.

## What they need in every message
- The date something takes effect, in their time zone.
- Whether it changes any numbers already reported, and by roughly how much.
- What, if anything, they need to do, and by when.

## Glossary

| We say | They hear / prefer | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "Fix is done" | Live in production, numbers corrected | Engineering meant "merged, not deployed." See breakdown 1. |
| "Sync" | Unclear; prefers "the nightly order import into the ledger" | Asked "which sync?" twice. |
| "Minor data issue" | Nothing to worry about | Understated a reconciliation gap. See breakdown 2. |
| "Sprint" / "backlog" | Not their vocabulary | Use "this two-week cycle" and "the queue." |

## Tone
- Direct and specific reads as respectful. One clear ask beats a friendly preamble.
- Exclamation points and "quick ask!" during close read as not understanding their workload.

## Friction points
- New requests in the last three business days of the month, unless urgent and labeled as such.
- Being told about a data problem after it shows up in their reports, rather than before.

## Past breakdowns
1. **What was said:** "The duplicate order fix is done, should be good now."
   **What they understood:** Duplicates were removed from the ledger.
   **What it cost:** Closed the month on uncorrected numbers; two days of rework.
   **Rule going forward:** Say "merged," "deployed," and "data corrected" as separate steps with dates. Never "done" alone.
2. **What was said:** "Heads up, minor data issue with yesterday's orders."
   **What they understood:** No action needed.
   **What it cost:** About $12K in orders missing from a daily report went unnoticed for a week.
   **Rule going forward:** Give the size of the impact in orders and dollars, even as an estimate.

## Writing samples
- "Before I sign off on close: is anything still changing in October?"
- "Can you give me a number, even a rough one?"
- "What do you need from me and by when?"
